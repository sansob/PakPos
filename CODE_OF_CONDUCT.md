# Code of Conduct

PakPos follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussions about the work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and
discriminatory language are not tolerated, in English, Bahasa Indonesia or any
other language.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately
through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/sansob/PakPos/security/advisories/new)
(marked "conduct") or by direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that
violate this code.
